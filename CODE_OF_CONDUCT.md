# Code of Conduct

We aim for a welcoming, technically rigorous community. Be respectful, assume good faith, critique claims and methods rather than people, and make space for newcomers. Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove violating content, close discussions, or restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub's available contact channels; do not include sensitive personal details in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports fairly, limit access to the information, and explain outcomes when appropriate.
